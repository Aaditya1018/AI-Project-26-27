# SafeStack AI — Curated 10-Behavior Damage-Risk Taxonomy

| # | Behavior Code | Common Industry Name | Temporal / Kinematic Signature | Baseline Risk Tier | Operational Impact | Recommended Prevention Nudge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | `BEH_DROP` | **Product Dropped from Height** | Vertical acceleration $a_y \ge 8.5\text{ m/s}^2$, freefall drop height $h > 0.5\text{m}$, impact energy $E_k > 45\text{J}$. | **Critical** | Shattered internal components, PCB fractures, dented chassis. | Enforce two-person buddy lift for items $>15\text{kg}$; inspect conveyor discharge lip. |
| **02** | `BEH_DRAG` | **Floor Dragging Cartons** | Sustained horizontal displacement $> 1.2\text{m}$ with zero vertical clearance directly along concrete dock floor. | **High** | Bottom cardboard tearing, dust infiltration, seal failure. | Equip loading bay with hydraulic pallet dollies and roller tracks. |
| **03** | `BEH_IMPROPER_STACK` | **Inverted Pyramid Stacking** | High center of gravity: heavier/larger crate placed atop lighter fragile packaging, tilt lean $\theta > 12^\circ$. | **Critical** | Structural crush of base cartons, sudden pallet collapse. | Restack placing highest-density cartons at the base foundation. |
| **04** | `BEH_PALLET_OVERHANG` | **Unsafe Pallet Overhang & Tilt** | Carton extends $> 25\%$ outside pallet perimeter boundary; dynamic lean angle $\theta > 14^\circ$. | **High** | Corner tear during forklift transit, trailer side-wall impact. | Shift load flush within pallet edges; secure with stretch film. |
| **05** | `BEH_ROUGH_HANDLING` | **Rough Handling / Slamming** | Sudden landing deceleration $|a| > 14\text{ m/s}^2$ without controlled lowering phase. | **High** | Shock damage to fragile glassware, unseated electronics connectors. | Train team on controlled two-point landing; use rubber dock shock mats. |
| **06** | `BEH_TOSS_THROW` | **Carton Tossing / Throwing** | Parabolic airborne projectile arc ($0.3\text{s} - 1.2\text{s}$) with no human limb contact during flight. | **Critical** | Corner impact burst, ruptured packaging, projectile injury. | Enforce positive hand-to-hand parcel transfer; install gravity chutes. |
| **07** | `BEH_STEP_ON_CARGO` | **Stepping or Sitting on Cartons** | Human bounding box positioned directly atop cargo bounding box with downward vertical contact load. | **Critical** | Complete carton collapse, puncture from footwear. | Provide rolling safety step stools; reiterate zero-stepping policy. |
| **08** | `BEH_OVERSTACK` | **Over-Stacking Height Limit** | Stack height exceeds safe clearance envelope ($> 2.1\text{m}$), obstructing sprinkler clearance or trailer ceiling. | **Medium** | Top box crushing against door lintel during transit. | Limit stack to 4 layers ($< 1.9\text{m}$ total height). |
| **09** | `BEH_ROLLING_TUMBLE` | **Rolling / Tumbling Cartons** | Sequential $90^\circ$ angular orientation inversions along floor instead of vertical lift and carry. | **Medium** | Corner scuffing, liquid content agitation or leakage. | Utilize 4-wheel platform carts; keep cargo oriented upright. |
| **10** | `BEH_FORKLIFT_PINCH` | **Forklift / Pedestrian Hazard** | Moving forklift mast within $1.5\text{m}$ proximity of pedestrian in loading bay corridor. | **Critical** | Severe personnel crush hazard, load tip-over on evasive turn. | Enforce mandatory 3-meter pedestrian blue-light safety zone. |
